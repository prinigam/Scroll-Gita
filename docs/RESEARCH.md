# Product reference research

Researched 2026-10-27. Public publisher/store claims, not installed-app testing. Apple screenshot image URLs exposed through the browsing tool were placeholders; Google image fetches failed. Exact screen layouts, onboarding sequence and live paywall offers remain unverified.

## Correct reference

[Scroll The Bible — Apple ID 6756558351](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/scroll-the-bible/id6756558351), developer Gabriel Jacobson, seller JCBSN LLC. Avoid conflating similarly named Scroll/Scrolls products. [Publisher](https://scrollthebibleapp.com/) confirms free reading/translation switching and optional paid study. [Product overview](https://www.thejcbsnbrand.com/apps/scroll-the-bible) confirms verse paging and saved position.

The Apple listing describes tap-to-study, original-language exploration, simplified explanations, related verses, Discover and search. Release notes add plans, widgets, completion, daily devotionals, comparison and auto-scroll. Its public catalog has multiple subscription SKUs; live offer eligibility cannot be inferred from that catalog.

[Privacy policy](https://www.thejcbsnbrand.com/apps/scroll-the-bible/privacy) describes local storage, bundled KJV, remotely requested additional translations, AI processing and marketing measurement. This describes their implementation, not a requirement to adopt their architecture.

## Existing Gita competitor

[Scroll The Gita — Apple](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/scroll-the-gita/id6813779163) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.scrollthegita.app), Triangle IT Solutions LLC. Listings advertise offline 700 verses, Sanskrit with seven language choices, study, guide, saved verses, streaks, reminders and sharing. Its existence does not change the user's intent to build this category of product. Existing use means our internal working name is not a cleared public brand.

## Our interpretation

Core value: quick entry into scripture, manageable text, immediate optional understanding and reliable continuation. Implement this functional model using original work, approved Gita content and an offline-first architecture. Do not infer proprietary technology, content rights, exact screen behavior, or verified performance from marketing pages.
